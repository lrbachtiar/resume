# Journal hero image credits

Hero images for the "Learning to Taste" Journal series, served from `/site/journal/`
and referenced by each post's `hero_image_url` in the seed.

## Original artwork
- **`flavour-wheel.svg`** — Beanmarkd original, built from our own flavour families +
  palette. *Not* derived from the SCA Coffee Taster's Flavor Wheel (that artwork is
  licensed **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0** — non-commercial + no-derivatives — so it cannot be used
  or recoloured here). The article cites the SCA/World Coffee Research wheel as the
  conceptual source in text only.
