Weird and Wonderfully Kiwi

These courses won't top the world rankings. They do something better.

✈️ Shares a runway

Poverty Bay Golf Club

Gisborne · Parkland

Fairways cross the active Gisborne Airport. A siren sounds when aircraft are on approach and golfers step aside. Entirely normal.

🐦 Gannet colony next door

Muriwai Golf Links

Auckland · Links

Thousands of Australasian gannets nest metres from the clifftop fairways. The birds are largely unbothered by your slice off the 8th.

♨️ Geothermal steam vents

Wairakei International

Taupo · Parkland

Pipes and steam vents from the Wairakei geothermal power station are visible from several holes. Unusual penalty areas apply.

🏚️ Flooded town beneath

Cromwell Golf Course

Central Otago · Inland

Holes trace the shore of Lake Dunstan, the reservoir that swallowed the original Cromwell township when Clyde Dam closed in 1992.

⛏️ Gold rush hazards

Arrowtown Golf Club

Otago · Parkland

Est. 1883. Natural hazards include 19th-century sluice tailings from the gold rush era. The Arrow River is also very much in play.

🌿 Hidden in the dunes

Te Arai Links

Northland · Links

Tucked behind Te Arai Beach with almost no signage. Tom Doak design that barely announces itself — you have to know it exists.
